? Help & calculation notes

Workbook source: Version 22 July 2026. The calculation constants and active formulas are reproduced in the JavaScript engine.

Round glass: enter the widest diameter.

Rectangular glass: enter length and width; the rounded diagonal becomes the effective diameter.

Heating history: repeated firing receives a longer, more conservative transformation time.

Bubble soak: unavailable for slump processes because slump temperature is below the softening point.

FullFuse and dams: effective annealing thickness may be recalculated to 6 mm, matching the workbook logic.

Always test valuable projects with sample glass and use kiln-manufacturer guidance.
